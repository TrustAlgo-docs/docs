### 100% Non-Custodial
The protocol is designed around a strict mathematical security constraint. By operating through Hyperliquid APIs, the system exclusively requires **"Read"** (data parsing) and **"Execute"** (order routing) permissions.

The "Withdraw" function is mathematically disabled at the exchange level, making it impossible for TrustAlgo servers, or anyone accessing them, to move funds outside the original wallet.

### Execution Engines
The architecture is divided into two operational layers:
* **Master Bots:** Isolated analytical units that process data flows for BTC, ETH, and SOL in separate and independent environments.
* **Router:** The central distribution node that receives signals from the Master Bots and executes simultaneous order routing on the market via ultra-low latency WebSocket connections.
